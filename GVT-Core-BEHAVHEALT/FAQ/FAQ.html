Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions (Program Workers & Managers)

Caseload

How do I add a client to my caseload?
Use Client Search/Add to find the client you are wanting to add to your caseload. On the search results screen, there will be a column called "Case Load Functions" and in that column, link to "Add/View Caseload" When you click this link, the client will be added to your caseload, and you will be redirected to your caseload screen.
How can I view a list of clients with upcoming due dates?
On your main dashboard, there is a section called, "Client Workflow/To Do List" that should show all clients on your caseload with forms due in the next few days or next week. There is a set number of days prior to the due date that you will be alerted.
How do I remove a client from my caseload?
In the client's row on your caseload, next to the client menu icon, there is an icon that is a red circle with an x inside of it. Click on that red x button and it will remove the client from your caseload.

Client Search & Add

How do I search for a client?

On the top left of the screen, next to the Home Button, there is a "FAMCare" icon. Click this icon and you will see a drop down of items. Towards the bottom is a link for "Client Search/Add" Clicking this link will take you to the search screen.

"

*Note: You can add the Search Page to your favorites by clicking on "Add to Favorites" at the top of the Search Page.*

I think I found the client, but their name appears to be spelled incorrectly, what should I do?
Check the date of birth and other demographic fields, to ensure it is indeed the same client. Then notify the data team if you think any client name or demographic information needs to be corrected.

Pathways and Pathway Forms

What is the client's Pathway?

The client's pathway is essentially the BHN program in which they are enrolled. Each pathway (or program) has a specific set of client forms that need to be completed. The client's pathway is set at enrollment.

"
What if the actual date I am entering the form is different from the date I spoke with the client? What should I enter for the Pathway Date on the form?

As a general rule, you should enter the date that you actually spoke with the client.

"
Are there other ways to access my Pathway Forms?

It is recommended that staff access the master pathway Dashboard by clicking on the Pathway Icon and then accessing the forms from the dashboard. Staff may also access forms that are currently on their Client Workflow/To Do List, by clicking on the Form Name (Description Column) The Master Pathway Dashboard can also be accessed through the Client Menu, by either clicking on the List Icon or from the FAMCare icon at the top of the screen. .

"
Will ineligible clients be assigned to a Pathway?

Yes, all clients enrolled will be assigned to a Pathway, but this does not require that all Pathway forms be completed. For instance, if a client is ineligible, they will still be added to a Pathway before dismissal.

"

Manager-Specific Questions

Pathway Forms

How do I access the forms I need to complete for my clients?
Once the client has been enrolled in your Program's Pathway, you will be able to access all of the relevant forms. If the client is already on your case load, you can click on the Pathway Icon, which will take you to the Master Pathway Dashboard. From the Dashboard, you click on the bar next to the due date for each form.

Troubleshooting

I see the client on my caseload, but I do not see the Pathway Icon. How do I access their forms?

In the caseload section, find your client and:

  1. Verify that you have a column on your dashboard called, "Pathway Workflow".
  2. If that column exists and you don't see the colorful icon, the client may not have been enrolled in your program yet OR this client may have already completed all of the pathway forms.
